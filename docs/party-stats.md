# Fixture party, level 1

Standard array is 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8, each used once. Proficiency bonus at level 1 is +2. Hit points are the maximum hit die plus Constitution modifier. Hill dwarves add 1. Ability modifier is `(score - 10) // 2`. Spell save DC is `8 + proficiency + ability modifier`. Spell attack is `proficiency + ability modifier`.

`equipment_choice: "default"` takes option (a) of each class and background choice. A choice of "any item from a category" takes the first entry in that SRD category (martial weapons start with the battleaxe; holy symbols start with the amulet). Packs are unpacked into their contents. A versatile weapon uses its one-handed damage. The first non-light melee weapon is equipped in the main hand.

## Tor, hill dwarf fighter

Base scores STR 15, DEX 12, CON 14, INT 8, WIS 13, CHA 10. Hill dwarf adds CON +2 and WIS +1.

- Final: STR 15 (+2), DEX 12 (+1), CON 16 (+3), INT 8 (−1), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 10 (+0)
- HP: 10 + 3 + 1 dwarven toughness = 14
- AC: chain mail 16 + shield 2 + Defense fighting style 1 = 19
- Speed: 25
- Saves: STR +4, DEX +1, CON +5, INT −1, WIS +2, CHA +0
- Skills: Athletics +4, Perception +4, Insight +4 (acolyte), Religion +1 (acolyte)
- Passive Perception: 10 + 4 = 14
- Battleaxe +4, 1d8+2 slashing. Light crossbow +3, 1d8+1 piercing.

## Mira, high elf wizard

- Final: STR 8 (−1), DEX 16 (+3), CON 13 (+1), INT 16 (+3), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 10 (+0)

- AC: 10 + 3 = 13 (no armor)
- Speed: 30

- Skills: Arcana +5, Investigation +5, Insight +3, Religion +5, Perception +3 (elf)
- Passive Perception: 10 + 3 = 13
- Spell DC 13, spell attack +5, two 1st-level slots
- Quarterstaff +1, 1d6−1 bludgeoning
- Cantrips: fire bolt, light, mage hand, plus prestidigitation for the high elf cantrip
- Spellbook and prepared list are the four prepared spells magic missile, shield, sleep, and mage armor, out of six known 1st-level spells
